Frequently asked questions

Articles aren't loading — what should I do?

Check your internet connection and pull to refresh. If the feed still won't load, please email us with your device model and iOS version so we can look into it.

How do I save an article?

Open an article and tap the bookmark icon. Saved articles are stored on your device and are available from the Saved tab.

Why do I see ads?

Car News is free to use and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. See our Privacy Policy for details on how ads work.

How do I delete my data?

Car News doesn't require an account and doesn't store personal data on our servers. Saved articles live only on your device — deleting the app removes them. See the Privacy Policy for more.
